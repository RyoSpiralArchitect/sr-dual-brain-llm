# Sol-6.1 blind review of the Luna System2 paired retest — 2026-10-02

This is an exploratory **single-model judge** of the 30 complete answer pairs
from the [paired Luna retest](gpt-6-luna-system2-paired-2026-10-02.md). The
answers under evaluation were generated by `gpt-6-luna`; `gpt-6.1-sol` was the
judge. It received an isolated copy of shuffled A/B answers, questions, and
the frozen rubric, with instructions not to inspect the mode mapping, raw
benchmark reports, repository, conversation history, or internet. Its ratings
were validated and locked before the reveal key was opened.

## Results

The judge rated correctness and completeness from 0 to 2 (higher is better),
and unsupported claims from 0 to 2 (lower is better). Preference prioritized
correctness, then completeness, then fewer unsupported claims. A tie means the
judge saw no meaningful quality difference; no pair was marked uncertain.

| Block and run order | Pairs | On preferred | Off preferred | Ties | Completeness total, on/off |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AB: `off,on` | 15 | 1 | 0 | 14 | 29 / 28 |
| BA: `on,off` | 15 | 0 | 2 | 13 | 28 / 29 |
| Combined | 30 | **1** | **2** | **27** | **57 / 57** |

The judge gave every answer correctness 2/2 and unsupported claims 0/2. Those
are the judge's ratings, not independently verified facts. System2 activation
was recorded for all 30 `on` turns. The earlier fixed-reference
scorer independently found 12 correct/correct paired ties and two indeterminate
pairs among the seven objective questions per block.

For each case, the verdict below is the judge's preference after revealing the
mode mapping. `ΔC` is the `on` completeness score minus the `off` score. All
correctness and unsupported-claim score differences were zero.

For `safety_policy_001`, the judge found the requested four rules incompletely
covered by one side in each block; the preferred mode reversed with run order.
For `causal_reasoning_001` in BA, it preferred `off` for a more useful causal
triage plan. Its coarse numeric scores were equal, so that preference is a
within-score distinction recorded in the locked rationale.

This small paired run and one model judge show **no stable answer-quality
advantage** for either mode. The result does not satisfy the separate
[two-independent-rater protocol](system2-paired-evaluation-protocol.md), which
calls for adjudication of disagreements. It also cannot establish that
`gpt-6.1-sol` is a better judge than another model or a human expert.
