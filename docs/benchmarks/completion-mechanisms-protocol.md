## Freeze and question selection

any provider output. The five deterministic cases are newly seeded and have not
appeared in the earlier frontier or replanning studies: two Boolean entailment

## Arms and limits

All four arms use the production native dual-brain path with an explicit left
lead, manual System2 ON, task goals AUTO, completion replanning AUTO, balanced
AUTO gates, Director/executive OFF and no ordinary continuation. The only
arm changes are `DUALBRAIN_COMPLETION_WORK_PRODUCTS` and
`DUALBRAIN_CRITIC_COMPLETION_AWARE`: both OFF, work product only, critic aware
only, and both ON. Use the five precommitted orders in the manifest. Sessions
use matching IDs, RNG seeds and logical clocks, but initial provider samples
are independently generated. No model answer, critic or action is replaced.

Request `gpt-6-luna` with provider-default effort. Native draft and ordinary
integration caps are 4096; integration after a 1024-cap work product is 3072
to stay within the ordinary-left allocation. Critic and repair caps are 2048.
Assert the actual serialized cap for every request before provider admission. Each
trial has an all-provider offered output-ceiling allowance of 12288 and an
ordinary-left allocation of 8192. There are at most 80 total admissions across
the 20 trials, no additional phase, paid judge, probe, retry of the campaign,
or outcome-driven tuning. Reserve caps without refund for failure, cancellation
or malformed completion. On an unclassified transport/auth/quota/provider
failure, stop future paid admissions. Keep cancellations and missing usage as
unknown, except proven pre-HTTP expiries.

## Assessment and replay

Use the frozen exhaustive Boolean, SQLite/Python, event-state and source-scope
oracles. Preserve strict JSON, duplicate-key, schema, and SQL bag rules. Run
only the evaluator-authored SQLite query, never model code. Report all 20
cells, including incomplete and failed cells. A terminally complete returned
answer retains its semantic score when external review is unavailable; report
workflow validity separately. Count the actual work-product and critic exposure,
initial-to-final semantic repairs/regressions and incomplete-to-complete
recoveries. Report exact and mean item scores with all six arm pairs per case.
If transport stops or the call cap prevents full coverage, do not rank a full
five-task comparison.

One exact replay at the unchanged frozen source must forbid provider HTTP and
match request/event coverage, canonical rows, grades, reservations, usage and
diagnostics. Replay recorded durations; only the actual owner may cancel a
request. Retain a replay failure without changing timers, policy or tape to
force success. Publish a filtered report and hashes. Raw questions, answers,
reviews, payloads and journals stay private in ignored `samples` directories.

These authored tasks and independent initial samples support descriptive
observations, not statistical superiority or an isolated causal effect of
either switch. A work-product shape receipt is not semantic verification.
