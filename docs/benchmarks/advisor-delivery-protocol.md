## Comparison and invariants

Compare `full_v4` with `compact_delivery` in real retained production sessions.
Both retain the confirmed-recovery v4 ledger and all twelve canonical module
modes in AUTO. The intervention changes Director pre-answer steering and metrics
post-answer monitoring delivery only. Ordinary Executive advice keeps its full
contract in both arms. Compact output is capped at **256 tokens** (or a lower
caller-configured cap), without continuations. The compact Director contract
contains only a memo of at most 240 characters, sparse operational control
fields and confidence; compact metrics contains only memo and confidence.
Effective configuration includes an opaque contract version. Compact request,
parse and output bounds are fixed and included in the source/configuration fingerprint before measurement; a
contract change must not reuse an unrelated effective latency profile.

Director's 1,600 ms wait and metrics' 2,500 ms wait remain unchanged, capped by a
real 120-second TurnDispatcher deadline. Executive still overlaps drafting and
consultation; its later wait is not its launch budget. Caller modes, manual
ON/OFF authority, cheap steering, source/evidence/security contracts, cancellation
ownership and compatibility defaults stay. No runtime classifier, extra recovery
request, timeout extension or AUTO-default promotion is introduced.

Any locally derived guidance must identify its actual provenance and use only
available facts. It cannot claim a model completion, persisted preference,
configuration change, completed operation or successful check that was never
observed. Report local and external exposure separately if both are present.

## Independent natural sequence

Retain one initially empty EngineSession per arm for five new questions. Use
left lead, System2 auto/balanced, evaluative dial, Executive observe and observer
both. Questions, initial state, caller modes and per-turn policy seeds match;
ordinary primary drafts, sources, learner and unconscious state then evolve
independently. Do not seed, clone or transplant latency/history. No external
records or PostgreSQL are seeded; record context is disabled, while ordinary
source updates and support calls count.

After both initial turns drain, idle for 16 real seconds, then alternate arm
order at each turn and otherwise proceed immediately. Phase names are labels,
not assumptions that provider latency or recovery confidence reaches a planned
state. Keep all planned cells even if a role is inhibited, times out or returns
invalid/empty guidance. A first success is not stable recovery evidence.

| Task | Explicit answer obligations | Material steering/omission risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-off parallel job | Two sentences; six-minute ideal time and one assumption | Job-only context must not become a general preference |
| Rollback readiness | Two sentences; reject inference and name a concrete missing check | Preserve requested independent cross-check and unknown rehearsal outcome |
| Updated incident retention | Two sentences; current 24-hour rule supersedes seven days | Keep current working context while rejecting stale facts and global preference claims |
| Temporary log access | Three bullets; scoped read-only permission, expiry/revocation and audit | Preserve independent privilege check and every material access control |
| Uncertain payment | Two sentences; unknown outcome and one duplicate-safe next step | A timeout is not completed payment evidence; preserve duplicate protection |

The independent-cross-check requests in the rollback and access cases are human
task requirements, not benchmark-only hidden obligations. The fixture's
`advice_obligations` specify only each role's relevant controls or caveats;
advisors need not repeat the complete technical answer. Memory steering does not
prove any preference was persisted or removed. Post-answer monitoring may
inspect supplied final-answer snippets and report fields; it cannot infer actual
storage, access grants, tests or payment outcomes from those fields alone.

## Quality, usefulness and omission assessment

Blind grade every available final answer and natural primary draft using the
existing task, grounding and clarification rubric, shuffled labels, strict
schemas and literal quote validation. Every required fact, caveat and output
format appears in the question. Omission may fail task while leaving asserted
claims grounded. Do not require an unrequested extra recommendation or treat an
unavailable draft/final as a passing answer.

Capture completed external advisor memo/control text in benchmark-only wrappers
before cancellation. Exclude late/error fallback text and keep raw text private.
Canonicalize role/text ordering before deterministic label shuffling. Assessment
packets contain question, reference, corresponding final answer, role, actual
role-specific input signals/observer facts, and the fixture's role-specific
obligations, but hide arm, delivery policy, profile,
latency, confidence and admission labels.
The supplied facts match the provider's actual input windows: at most 1,200
characters of Director signals JSON and 1,600 characters of role context.
Unsent/truncated facts cannot justify a grade.

Separately assess **relevance**, **grounding**, **potential_use**, and
**critical_coverage**. Coverage checks only that role's material control or
caveat; it does not require a miniature complete answer. Generic tone/privacy
boilerplate alone cannot establish potential use. A compact output that drops a
required scope, verification request or uncertainty safeguard must fail coverage
even if its remaining text is relevant and true. Every pass/fail needs a literal
nonempty quote from the advice text; a coverage-failure quote identifies the
existing incomplete guidance, not nonexistent omitted words. Use uncertain when
the supplied data cannot support a judgment. Retain malformed grades as grader
failures, without paid retry.

Metrics observations occur after the answer and cannot improve that already
returned answer. Grade monitoring potential and visible coverage only, respecting
the supplied snippet limit; unobserved external outcomes remain unknown. Model
potential-use grades do not establish causal downstream benefit or authoritative
truth. Distinguish structural nonempty completion, content quality, omitted
controls and final-answer quality.

Report requested opportunities, starts, available/structurally usable outputs,
graded outputs, criterion pass/fail/uncertain counts and missing exposure by role.
Missing or timed-out advice is absent exposure, not a content pass or content
failure. Include time/turns to first usable output, subsequent timeouts, full
probe/confirmation and induced ordinary work. A shorter output that is never
delivered has no measured usable content exposure.

## Paid budget and replay

Production uses `gpt-6-luna`; blind final/draft and advice judges use `gpt-6-sol`.
The base and final/draft judge configuration is 2,048 output tokens; the separate
four-criterion advice judge has a fixed **4,096 output-token cap** to cover up to
six outputs per packet. All have a 60-second provider timeout and no continuations.
Compact role-specific bounds and schemas are frozen
in production source before calls, and effective request options are recorded.
The hard maximum is **96 HTTP attempts total**: ten full turns, all primary,
support, repair, consultation, recovery, confirmation and induced ordinary work,
five final/draft judge packets and up to five advice judge packets. No free
warmup, resampling or paid replay. Transport/auth/quota failure blocks new paid
requests. Cancellation receipts remain unknown when unavailable; known partial
tokens cannot establish exact monetary savings.

Record actual resource reads and recovery claim/finish capabilities. Exact replay
uses original recorded facts and HTTP occurrences, never cached response latency
as fresh learning. Require matching statuses, answers, drafts, canonical advice,
both grade sets, full gates, normalized resource occurrences and source/pending
counts with zero fallback transport. Preserve mismatches: instantaneous recorded
cancellation does not reproduce a real timer, wall time, stop reason or
asynchronous completion order. Real owned-task/native-timeout tests separately
verify cancellation and no leaked work.

Raw artifacts remain under ignored
`samples/module-gating-advisor-delivery-*/`. Publish safe aggregates and hashes
without prompts, answer/advice text, judge quotes or credentials. One correlated
sequence per arm, any synthetic tests and model-based grades cannot justify
general latency, causal utility or AUTO-default claims. If the compact contract
still misses native windows, establish that feasibility limit rather than
extending waits or hiding failures.
